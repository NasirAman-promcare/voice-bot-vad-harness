# Consent to record your voice: AQIS Test Lab, campaign A

Consent reference (the lab fills this in): ca-c___

## Why we are recording
The AQIS Test Lab checks how well voice bot software works. We use your recordings for testing, benchmark measurement, and error diagnosis for voice bot pipelines. For example, we test how well software hears when you speak, writes down what you say and reads your tone of voice.

## What we record
You read about 80 short prompts aloud on your own phone. This takes about 30 minutes. Some prompts ask you to sound a certain way, such as pleased or upset. We record your voice and a few details of the session: your phone, the room, the background noise and your accent group. Your name is not stored with your recordings. They carry a speaker code instead.

## How we store your recordings
Your recordings are stored and processed only in Google Drive (AQIS_LAB), Google Colab, and Oracle Cloud VM. Audio is never stored on GitHub. The lab owner keeps this signed form offline, apart from your recordings.

## Who can see them
Only the AQIS test lab owner (@Nasir) and authorized project raters/evaluators can see or hear your recordings.
