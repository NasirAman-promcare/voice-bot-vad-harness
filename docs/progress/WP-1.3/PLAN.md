## Definitions the ruler tests fix
- **Target WER** is the pooled WER (total errors over total reference words) of the 6,300 target turns, the figure behind the 11.06% of Sprint 1's oracle (`docs/results/S1/oracle.csv`, "target set"). Sprint 1's headline per model is the mean of the six slice WERs (qwen3-asr 16.55%) and is a different number; `cascade.csv` carries both (`wer`, `mean_slice_wer`), and the gate uses the pooled one.
- **Escalation rate** is turns sent to the heavy model over all turns, never above the rate asked: `floor(rate x turns)` turns, the highest gate scores first, ties by a seeded hash.
- **Cost saving** is GPU seconds from audio length over RTFx (Sprint 1 `speed.csv`): stage 1 for every turn plus the heavy model for escalated turns, against the heavy model alone. **Added latency** is the share of escalated turns times the heavy model's batch-1 p50 latency.
- **Hybrid gate** is a logistic regression on gate features, predicting that the heavy model saves at least one word error on the turn. Its scores are always out of fold by speaker (`test_s3_out_of_fold_scores_come_from_models_that_never_saw_the_row_or_its_cluster`).
- Each heavy model is simulated alone. "Qwen3 or Cohere" is not decided per turn by a realisable rule; the best-of-both figure is reported as an oracle bound (`best_two_heavy_oracle_wer` in `summary.json`).

## Where this build departs from the brief
1. **Confidence is not in the Sprint 1 files.** Their prediction files hold text, not token log-probabilities, so the gate's first feature cannot be simulated from them. The `confidence` action reruns parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v2 on the GPU with NeMo's token confidence switched on (a max-probability score and an entropy-based score) and saves them to `cascade/features.parquet`. NeMo's `confidence_cfg` calls are written from its documentation and **were not run**; the smoke run is their first test. Until it has run, the `confidence` and `entropy` policies are reported `unavailable` with the reason, and never filled in. The other features (duration, words per second, digit tokens, channel, Silero v5 non-speech ratio) come from the Sprint 1 files and audio.
2. **Words per second and digit tokens** come from stage 1's own hypothesis, which exists at gate time; nothing from the heavy model or the reference reaches the gate.
3. **Eka has no training split** (3,619 English turns, `test` only; checked on the Hugging Face card). The "Eka training split" is the part of Eka whose speakers are not in the 300-turn `medical` evaluation slice (see the closeout below: on the real data this became "whose turns are not in it"), with turns that have at least one scored entity. Up to 2,000 turns, spread over speakers, 10% of the speakers held out for early stopping; the training audio is rendered clean (40%), phone (40%) and noise_10 (20%) with the Sprint 1 conditions. The manifest counts how many evaluated drug names also appear in training, so a recall gain from names the model saw is visible as such.
4. **Prompt biasing terms** are the most frequent drug names in the Eka turns of the same non-evaluated speakers (at most 150 and 1,500 characters), never the evaluated slice's own entities. The `numbers` slice gets a one-sentence digit hint instead of a term list. Granite uses its card's keyword prompt; Qwen3-ASR uses `transcribe(context=...)`.
5. **LoRA recipe** (r 16, alpha 32, dropout 0.05, NF4 4-bit with bf16 compute, early stopping on validation loss with patience 3) follows Qwen's own `finetuning/qwen3_asr_sft.py` for the data format, forward patch and label masking. The target pattern covers `thinker.model.layers.*` attention and MLP projections only, not the audio tower. It stops with an error if the pattern matches no module. **Not run here**: module names, 4-bit loading through `Qwen3ASRModel.from_pretrained(quantization_config=...)`, `prepare_model_for_kbit_training` on this model and the merge for evaluation are untested; `train --smoke` is their first test. The base is Qwen3-ASR (the brief's other option, Parakeet, has no recipe here).
6. **Sprint 1's pinned revisions** (`s1/revisions.json`) are reused for stage 1, Granite and Qwen3-ASR, so the baselines and the new runs use the same weights.
7. **Smoke runs** (`--smoke`, CLAUDE.md rule 6) write to `<out>/smoke` and never touch the real files or checkpoints.
8. `report` writes `decision_inputs.md` (gate thresholds and gate results), not `DECISION.md`: the Go/No-Go decision and the end-to-end blueprint are the owner's and the reviewer's, as in Sprints 1 and 2.
9. As in the earlier sprints, libraries outside the phase 0 lock load through `aqis_lab.bench.lib()` so the two Phase 0 guard tests stay unchanged. GPU actions import no scorer (`test_s3_gpu_actions_import_no_scorer`).

## Follow-up: token confidences ran out of GPU memory (branch `s3/confidence-memory`, 27 Oct 2026)
The first Colab run of `confidence` saved the 300 turns of `us_conv/phone`, then stopped with `CUDA out of memory` (22 GB L4, 17 GB held by PyTorch) in the second file. What the log shows: NeMo accepted the confidence settings (frame and token confidence on, `max_prob` then `entropy`), so the call names in `configure()` were right; two causes were mine. (1) `preserve_alignments = True`, which NeMo warns "uses enormous amount of memory" because it keeps every logit; token confidence does not need it. (2) The decoding strategy was changed again for every batch of 64 turns, for each of the two methods. Fixed: alignments off, the decoding set up once per method per file, `cascade.confidence.batch_size: 16` in `s3.yaml`, the GPU cache freed after each pass, files that already have both scores skipped on a rerun, and a stop when NeMo returns no confidence for a whole file (an empty column would be reported as unavailable, never filled). Whether the token confidences are non-empty was not visible in the log; the run now prints the share of turns that have them. The `s3.yaml` hash changes. The re-run of `confidence` skips nothing it has not finished: `us_conv/phone` is already in `cascade/features.parquet`.

## Closeout: four fixes the Colab runs needed, committed to main (29 Oct 2026)
The Colab run used these changes before they were on main; main now reproduces it. Tests: `tests/aqis_lab/test_s3_closeout.py` (9 of 11 failed against the merged code).
1. **`confidence.configure()`** assigns `confidence_cfg` (and `method_cfg`) as a whole dictionary inside `open_dict` when the model's decoding config has none, instead of setting attributes of keys that do not exist (an OmegaConf error).
2. **`train_data.split_train()`**: the 300 evaluated `medical` turns were drawn from every Eka speaker, so leaving the evaluated speakers out left nobody to train on. When fewer than 4 speakers remain, only the evaluated *turns* are left out. **Consequence for reading the fine-tuning result:** the training speakers then overlap the evaluated speakers (the split note and `train/manifest.json` say so), which is the easier setting for a fine-tuned model. The measured drug recall (57.9% on phone) and the `us_conv` regression are therefore not a speaker-held-out estimate. Neither `docs/results/S3/DECISION.md` nor the scorecard states this; I did not edit them (owner-approved).
3. **`train_data.prepare()`** writes the training wav files to `<out>/train/audio` beside `train.jsonl` on Drive, so a dropped session keeps them (they were in scratch).
4. **`train_lora.patch_input_embeddings()`** gives `Qwen3ASRForConditionalGeneration` `get_input_embeddings` and `set_input_embeddings`, delegating to `thinker`; PEFT's `prepare_model_for_kbit_training` calls them. It runs after the forward patch and before the k-bit preparation.

What was and was not checked here: the four changes are covered by tests with stand-ins for NeMo's config, the Qwen model and Eka; the Colab run is the evidence that they work on the real stack. Config hash `4ac9f213`, the one on the scorecard, is unchanged by this closeout.

## Follow-up: turns with no stage 1 words (30 Oct 2026)
Found in an independent audit of the committed results: `policies.json` marked `confidence` and `entropy` unavailable ("missing for 46 of 6300 turns") and the hybrid gate ran without its confidence feature, so the gate's first feature never entered any policy; my own rule (a policy is unavailable if any turn lacks the feature) caused this, and my closeout wording ("every stage ran") overstated it. Fix: `simulate.impute_no_word_turns()` scores a turn for which stage 1 produced no words as least confident (confidence and entropy 0.0; the entropy feature is an entropy-based confidence, so 0.0 is maximum entropy). It applies to missing scores of word-less turns only: a turn with words and no score keeps the policy unavailable, nothing imputed is written to `cascade/features.parquet`, and `policies.json` counts the turns. Whether the 46 turns are all word-less is checked by the rerun itself: if any has words, the policy stays unavailable and says how many. Two further facts from the audit, unchanged by this fix: the `channel` policy has no score threshold (`thresholds.json` holds `inf`, because the telephone group is larger than the 35% budget, so its 14.25% is a seeded random 35% of telephone turns), and the oracle WER has no confidence interval in any file (the intervals are of differences). `DECISION.md` was corrected to say so and carries an open item until the rerun.
