# AUDIT — WP-0.4, round 1

Auditor: wp-auditor · Date: 2026-09-27 · Commit audited: 55f9b5137baa0beb277fb1b5eb5e3a5043b0c449 · Previous audited commit: first audit · Protocol: version 2

## Verdict: FAIL
CRITICAL: 1 · MAJOR: 1 (fixed 0, accepted as debt 0, open 1) · MINOR: 4

Most of the work is sound. I re-ran every acceptance command myself:
- Two builds and the committed pack are byte-identical.
- `pack verify` passes on the committed pack with the exact counts.
- The registry regenerates byte-identically from the source cache.
- The three MD5 pins equal OpenSLR's published checksum files, which I fetched myself.
- All 30 LibriSpeech clips in the pack hold exactly the samples of their source FLAC.
- The fast suite passes (1357), the slow suite passes (14), the gate reports 0 findings, and CI is green on `55f9b51`.
- All 24 of my must-catch breaks fail a named test, and all 10 exploratory breaks are caught.

It fails for one reason: **`pack verify` reports `OK` (exit 0) when a listed archive is present as a folder or a dangling symlink instead of a file** (WP-0.4-F01). In that case it neither reports nor checks any of that archive's clips. On the committed pack with one archive replaced this way, it prints `OK … tts 10` with all 10 tts clips absent. The same skip applies to `LICENCES.md` and `build_record.json`. This breaks FR-0.4-05 ("recomputes every hash") and must-catch 3 ("fails on any … missing … member") for an input that needs no editing of the pack's contents: a Drive symlink on Colab when Drive is not mounted is enough. A second, related verify gap (F02) needs a crafted manifest and is MAJOR.
